# Privacy Policy – drop on drop

**Last updated: September 2026**

drop on drop ("the app") is developed by Erik as an independent project.

## Data We Collect

We collect **no personal data**. The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or any form of tracking.

## Data Stored on Your Device

Your habits, schedule, and completion history are stored:
- **Locally** on your device using shared app storage between the app and widget.
- **In your personal iCloud account** via iCloud Key-Value Storage, so your data syncs across your own devices. This data is managed entirely by Apple and is never accessible to us.

## In-App Purchases

The optional one-time purchase ("drop on drop Pro") is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to any payment information.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.
